About KISSTEE

Independent multi-vendor specialists

KISSTEE International Services Ltd is a certified B2B vertical mobility engineering firm and premier subcontracting partner for commercial, residential and industrial infrastructure across Nigeria.

We work across all major lift brands — delivering unbiased, high-performance solutions for real estate developers, facility managers, corporate hubs, private estates, hotels and hospitals.

With bases in Lagos and Akure, and full mobilisation across all 36 states plus cross-border projects, we provide consistent technical support and access to globally sourced parts.

Multi-Vendor Expertise Arkel, Metron, MLC, Fuji Electric, SEC and more — we choose the best fit for your project.
Turnkey Delivery From supply and shaft construction through installation, testing and handover — one accountable partner.
Long-Term Partnership 2-year warranty + 2-year free maintenance standard on every new installation.
What We Do

Scope of Services

KISSTEE International Services Ltd provides complete vertical mobility solutions for commercial, residential and industrial projects across Nigeria. From the supply of elevators and escalators through professional installation, testing and commissioning, to ongoing maintenance, spare parts, consultancy and shaft construction — we deliver end-to-end support with a standard 2-year warranty and 2-year free maintenance on new installations.
